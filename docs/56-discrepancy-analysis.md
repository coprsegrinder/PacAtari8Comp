# 56 - Discrepancy analysis (checklist stage 90)

Hand-written, 2026-11-23. Checklist stage 90: "Identify differences between the original and reconstructed software, investigate their causes, and implement corrections." This chapter is the **ledger**: every difference that was found between the original (the ROM, and the evidence of the real machine) and the port, or in the way the port was tested, with its cause and what was done. It was made by reading the whole register (`OPEN-VERIFICATION.md`, C-96 to C-125) for every finding, not from memory, and then by running a last sweep.

## 1. The result

- **No unexplained difference between the C port and the ROM remains.** The C port equals the ROM in the whole state, the ordered chip writes and the RANDOM reads after every frame on **709,116 frames** of games (the four recorded sessions, the random and edge games, the games played on purpose, the long games, and a final sweep of 60 new games: sections 4 and 5), and in the frame classes, the changes of the 20 state variables and the handler's routine counts on 171,300 frames (`53`, `55`).
- **Every defect found was in a tool, a test, a comment or a document, or a build on a system not tried before; none was a difference of behaviour of the game** (section 2). All are corrected and each has a test or a check that would catch it again.
- **The differences that remain are by decision or by the limit of the evidence** (section 3), each named with where it is recorded.
- **Two items stay open in the register** (section 6): OV-71 and OV-72. Neither is an unexplained difference: one is a comparison not yet made, the other is machines not available.

## 5. The final sweep

60 new games of the `mixed` profile (seeds 4 to 63, 6,000 frames each, about half for two players), none of which had been run before, compared with the ROM frame by frame: **63 games, 378,000 frames, 1,436,192 chip writes and 1,627 RANDOM reads compared, 0 differences** (`analysis/evidence-sweep-summary.txt`). The first pass found 2 of the 60 different, in `DliColors` only, at the frame where the attract values change and a trigger press starts a new game; the cause is a race of the original (`55` section 3, kind 4: five different `DliColors` for five neighbouring interrupt phases of the ROM harness, the C one of them), the generator now keeps triggers and keys out of the 3 frames before and 8 after a change of the attract values, and the sweep (and the three `mixed` games of stage 89, which that guard changed) was played again: all equal. The sweep reached no eaten pause or level end (random sticks do not: the games played on purpose of `53` and `55` do).

## 6. What stays open

- **OV-71**: routine call counts of the key loop, the new-game sequence and the display-list interrupts are not compared (the harness's idle passes would be counted); games to a high level and the 255-level wrap are not run. Neither is a known difference; they are comparisons not made.
- **OV-72**: macOS, MSVC, 32-bit, big-endian, ARM, other C libraries and the real front end were not available.

Neither blocks the next stages. Stages 91-100 (documentation, archive, GitHub) need the owner's approval step by step; stage J, the playable Windows 11 port, is locked until A-I are verified.
