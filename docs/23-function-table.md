## How to read the table

- **in**: registers and flags the routine reads before it has written them (its inputs); **clobbers**: what it may change, including through its callees, and does not give back; **saves and restores**: A, X or Y that it changes but restores (in a zero-page byte or on the stack) before every return; **out**: what at least one caller still needs after the call (the return value). A flag in `out` is a result in the flags (for example `C` after a lookup, or `Z` after a compare). Registers and flags that are neither in `in` nor in `clobbers` are preserved.
- **reads / writes**: the zero-page variables, tables, RAM and hardware registers the routine itself touches (callees not included; `,idx` = indexed, `(name),y` = through the pointer pair at `name`). **calls**: callees; **tail**: routines it jumps into (their code is part of this routine).
- **loops**: backward branches (a loop each). **exits**: RTS / RTI instructions.

## The calling convention (stage 39)

There is one convention and it is not written anywhere in the ROM; it follows from the 81 routines below:

- **No parameters on the stack.** Arguments go in **A, X, Y** and in **zero-page variables** (the pointers `SrcPtr` / `DstPtr`, `CurrentPlayer`, the shared tables indexed by X); results come back in **A, X, Y, the flags (C, Z, N)** and in memory. Routines are not re-entrant in general (shared scratch zero page).
- **X is the entity index** (ghost 0-3, Pac-Man 4) in the per-entity routines (`StepAndDrawEntity`, `GhostBehaviour`, `SteerToTarget`, `GhostSeesPacMan`, ...) and the player index in the per-player ones; the per-entity variables are arrays indexed by it (`EntityX,x`, `GhostState,x`, ...).
- **No register is preserved by convention.** A routine that changes a register and gives it back is marked 'saves and restores' (10 routines for X - mostly the per-entity routines that loop over ghosts -, 1 for Y, 2 for A: in a zero-page byte, or on the stack by `PHA ... PLA` in the interrupt handlers); every other routine may change A, X, Y and the flags, and a caller that needs a register after a call saves it itself.
- Counts over the 81 routines: input A 5, X 26, Y 5, flags 0; result in A 7, X 1, Y 1, flags 1; 50 routines read no register at entry.
